MUSE° · PRIVACY

Your listening stays yours.

Last updated: August 2, 2026

MUSE does not require an account, sell personal information, run advertising trackers, or collect analytics. Favorites and preferences are stored on your device.

Information MUSE handles

  • Search terms are sent to Apple's public iTunes Search API to return music and video previews.
  • Audio and video previews, artwork, and store links are streamed or loaded from Apple-hosted services.
  • Favorites, theme choices, and playback preferences are stored locally on your device or browser.
  • Issue-report drafts are stored locally. When you choose to send one, MUSE transmits the details you entered and optional diagnostics to the configured support destination or an app you select.

Information MUSE does not collect

MUSE does not operate an account system or analytics service. It does not request your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier. A contact email is optional and is used only to follow up on the issue you report.

Retention and deletion

Locally saved data remains until you clear it, reset the app, clear site/app storage, or uninstall MUSE. Search responses are cached briefly in memory for performance and are cleared when the app session ends. Submitted issue reports are retained only as long as reasonably needed to investigate, respond, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

Third-party services

Apple may process requests under its own privacy terms when MUSE uses the iTunes Search API or opens Apple Music. Review Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Changes

If MUSE adds accounts, analytics, advertising, or another data-processing service, this policy and the relevant store privacy disclosures must be updated before release.
